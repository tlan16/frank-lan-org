1. Who we are

franklan.org (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates franklan.org at franklan.org. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, use our services, or contact us.

For privacy questions or requests, contact:

  • Email: privacy@franklan.org

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our website, we collect the following information:

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, account details, and information submitted through forms, purchases, subscriptions, surveys, or support requests.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser and device details, pages viewed, referring pages, interactions with the website, and the date and time of visits.

If you choose not to provide information needed for a particular feature, we may be unable to provide that feature.

3. How we use information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Operating the website and providing requested services.
  • Creating and managing accounts, processing transactions, and providing customer support.
  • Responding to questions and sending service-related notices.
  • Understanding website usage and improving functionality and content.
  • Sending marketing communications where permitted and managing communication preferences.
  • Detecting fraud, preventing misuse, and maintaining security.
  • Meeting legal obligations and establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use computer technologies to compute.

5. When we disclose information

We disclose personal information in some following circumstances:

  • Service providers: to providers that support functions such as hosting, payments, analytics, communications, and customer support.
  • Legal and safety purposes: when legally required or otherwise permitted to protect rights, safety, or security, or to address fraud or misuse.
  • Business transactions: in connection with a proposed or completed merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale, subject to applicable protections.
  • At your direction: when you request or authorize a disclosure.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; obtain a copy in a portable format; restrict or object to certain processing; or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To make a request, contact privacy@franklan.org. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law and explain any relevant limits or exceptions.

10. Children’s privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact privacy@franklan.org so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. Third-party websites and services

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to review those policies before providing personal information.

12. Complaints

If you have concerns about our handling of personal information, contact privacy@franklan.org with details of your concern.

Depending on your location, you may also have the right to complain to a relevant privacy or data protection regulator.

13. Changes to this policy
